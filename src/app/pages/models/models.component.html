HumanGEO

Metabolic digital twin


Refs: [1. HumanGEMlib repository, 2. Application Note]


Digital software models liver, stomach, gastric, and intestinal health; collectively called metabolic health. Input data are kidney function test (KFT), liver function test (LFT), complete blood count (CBC), Hb1Ac level, insulin level, and urine routine reports. Trained to model metabolism over time on real patient data, HumanGEO latent representation holds mechanistic insight into metabolic dysregulation, liver fat deposition, pancreatic failure and digestive hormone dysregulation. Powered by HumanGEMlib Python library. Made by CTO Noorul Ali.

PPBag

Smart Urine Bag


Refs: [1. Early prototype video]


Smart urine bag for automated urine measurement and diagnostics. Gauge urine volume. Paper-based urine test. Digital report data in 1 hour. Bluetooth/WiFi connectivity.

STDinfo

AIDS Risk Quantifier


Refs: [1. Application Note, 2. STDinfo repository]


Software plugin to determine transmission risk of sexually transmitted diseases (STD) between 2 persons without sharing medical reports. Cryptographically secure to hide sensitive patient data. Made by CTO Noorul Ali.

Precision AMR

Tackle antimicrobial resistance (AMR) with pathogen sequencing, antimicrobial susceptibility and resistance drug identification and sequence logging of novel resistant pathogens. Designed by Shaikh Mohammad Omar Faruk.
