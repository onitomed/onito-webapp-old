What is ONITO ?

MEDICAL DATA STORAGE

ONITO stores all medical data for patients, including test reports, scans, prescriptions, and bedside monitor data points. The application stores medical data in an accessible format easily shared with doctors and medical staff.

HEALTHCARE DATA INFRASTRUCTURE

ONITO can be embedded in hospitals and doctor offices. ONITO replaces traditional data infrastructure for medical data storage by installing a physical ONITO system. The advantages of ONITO healthcare data infrastructure are interoperability and global access to complete patient records. Any doctor or hospital staff across the world can access any patient record in real-time on ONITO with an Internet connection and permission.

PRIVACY FOCUS

ONITO respects privacy of its users. Patient data are accessed only with permission from the patient. This feature is inbuilt into data sharing. Any data used by ONITO for doctor training is used after patient consent and anonymization. A patient record cannot be traced back to the patient after anonymization.

DOCTOR AID

Anonymization is the process of scrubbing a patient record of Personally Identifiable Information including name, date of birth, address, phone number, email, genetic sequence, and insurance information. Many anonymized patient records can train doctors to provide better care.
