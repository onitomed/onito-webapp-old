Tools on ONITO

AITP

Autoimmune Treatment Planner


Refs: [1. RA Atlas CITEseq repository, 2. Immunogenomics Cell-Gene Cluster]


Precision medicine tool for rheumatoid arthritis (RA) to recommend best drug treatment.

RA is a heterogenous autoimmune disease. Drugs work on some patients and fail on others. RA patients have different types of dysregulated immune cells, known as disease heterogeneity. Medicines for RA target specific causes of inflammation (Tumor Necrosis Factor-alpha, Interleukin 6, B cells) caused by specific dysregulated immune cells. Dysregulated immune cell type is not easily determined from traditional diagnosis, but synovium biopsy scRNAseq data of a patient processed with AITP machine learning model determines dysregulated immune cell types and numbers (abundance). AITP uses these immune cell types and numbers with known biologic drug mechanisms in a transparent and explainable rule-based approach to determine best drug treatment. Based on research by Soumya Raychaudhuri at Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, Boston, United States.

HUMANGEO

Metabolic digital twin


Refs: [1. Type 2 Diabetes Application Note]


Digital software models liver, stomach, gastric, and intestinal health; collectively called metabolic health. HumanGEO ingests kidney function test (KFT), liver function test (LFT), complete blood count (CBC), Hb1Ac level, insulin level, and urine routine reports as input. HumanGEO uses input as training data to model metabolism over time. Logic is to correct for real patient data leading to constraints for HumanGEO to run recorded metabolic states. When HumanGEO runs and reaches parameters equivalent to reported patient data, the underlying metabolic state in HumanGEO captures insight of active cellular mechanisms in metabolic dysregulation, liver fat deposition, pancreatic failure and digestive hormone dysregulation. Made by CTO Noorul Ali.

PPBAG

Smart Urine Bag


Refs: [1. Early prototype video]


Smart urine bag for automated urine measurement and diagnostics. Gauge urine volume. Paper-based urine test. Digital report in 1 hour. Bluetooth/WiFi connectivity.

STDinfo

AIDS Risk Quantifier


Refs: [1. Application Note, 2. STDinfo repository]


Software plugin to determine transmission risk of sexually transmitted diseases (STD) between 2 persons without sharing medical reports. Cryptographically secure to hide sensitive patient data. Made by CTO Noorul Ali.
