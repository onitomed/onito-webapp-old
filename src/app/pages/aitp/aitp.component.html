AITP uses synovium single-cell RNA sequencing data from patients with rheumatoid arthritis (RA). AITP analyzes uploaded data to rank immune cell type abundance and determine cell-type abundance phenotype. AITP finally recommends drugs based on cell-type abundance phenotype to specifically target immune cells with maximum dysregulation. AITP chooses from the RA armamentarium of 14 FDA-approved drugs.
